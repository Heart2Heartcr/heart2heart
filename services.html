Most care options along the Central Pacific fall into two categories: home health agencies focused on medical tasks, or informal caregivers without real training or accountability. Heart to Heart is neither. We are a private, non-medical in-home care and lifestyle support company built around one outcome — protecting the life someone has already built.

Most families need a blend of the support below, not a single category. Every plan is individualized, and we help you find the right mix in one conversation.

Our services

What we help with.

Personalized Daily Living

Day-to-day support that protects comfort, confidence, and independence at home — steady help that never feels like it's taking over.

Memory & Cognitive Support

Patient, consistent care for clients living with dementia, Alzheimer's, or memory changes — the same calm faces, day after day.

Recovery & Transitional Support

Lifestyle & Wellness

Family Peace of Mind

Transparent communication and attentive observation that keeps families, near and far, confidently informed.

Why it works

Individualized care isn't a nicer word for the same service.

Before any care begins, we spend time understanding a person's lifestyle, preferences, personality, and goals — then build a plan around that specific person, not a standard checklist. Because needs change, so do our plans; we reassess regularly, so families never wonder whether the support still fits.
