Meet Felicia

The person behind the care.

Heart to Heart wasn't built by a committee. It was built by someone who understands what your family is going through.

When I first moved to Costa Rica, I was building a new life for my two children and myself, far from the family I had always relied on. I understood, in a very personal way, what it feels like to start over — to create a sense of home from nothing.

Then I met a client who became so much more than that word suggests. My children saw him as their grandfather, and he embraced them the way grandfathers do. Somewhere in that friendship, I learned something I have carried into every year of this work since.

"Care isn't a task you complete. It's a relationship you build."

I have come to believe that if we are fortunate enough to grow old, we live through two remarkable seasons of vulnerability — our first years, and our last. Both deserve patience. Both deserve dignity. Both deserve to be surrounded by people who see us, not just our needs.

Retirement was never meant to be the end of the story. It's another season — one that so many of our clients crossed an ocean to enjoy. Needing a little help should never mean giving up independence, dignity, purpose, or joy. It should simply mean having someone in your corner who helps you keep all of it.

So when I think about why Heart to Heart exists, it isn't complicated. We don't replace independence. We protect it. We show up so that mornings on the terrace stay mornings on the terrace, so that visits from grandchildren stay visits from grandchildren, so that the life someone came here to build keeps being lived, not just maintained.

Because the best chapters of life deserve to be fully lived.

With gratitude,
Felicia
Founder, Heart to Heart

Meet Taylor

Registered Respiratory Therapist & Caregiver

Taylor has called Costa Rica home for nearly six years, building a life rooted in community, connection, and caring for others. As a Registered Respiratory Therapist with years of experience supporting people and families through some of life's most vulnerable moments, she brings both professional knowledge and a caregiver's heart to Heart to Heart. Taylor is also an active caregiver within the company, providing the same personalized, respectful support she would want for someone in her own family.

As Heart to Heart's Registered Respiratory Therapist, Taylor guides care planning and caregiver training — an added layer of professional observation and communication for every client, never medical treatment or physician care.
