For healthcare & referral partners

A trusted next step for your patients.

A professional, reliable partner for safe discharge planning — coordinated closely with families and your team.

Message us on WhatsApp

Recovery at home should never come with unnecessary risk. When a client is discharged after a hospital stay, illness, or procedure, Heart to Heart builds a short-term, attentive care plan around a clear goal: healing safely, without losing ground during recovery.

That means help with mobility, meals, and getting to follow-up appointments — from caregivers who understand that the goal isn't indefinite care, but a safe, steady return to independence. We coordinate closely with families and discharge planners so nothing falls through the cracks in those first critical weeks at home.

Professional oversight

Caregivers guided by real clinical fluency.

Every caregiver is guided by experienced healthcare professionals, including a Registered Respiratory Therapist trained in advanced emergency response. It's an added layer of professional observation and communication for every client — never medical treatment or physician care.
