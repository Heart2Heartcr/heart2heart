Recovery & Transitional Support

From hospital to home, with confidence.

Short-term, attentive support that helps clients heal safely at home after a hospital stay, illness, or a diagnosis such as stroke or Parkinson's — built around a finish line.

Message us on WhatsApp

The hardest part often isn't the hospital. It's the first few weeks back home — when a fall, a missed medication, or simply doing too much too soon can undo real progress.

Recovery is not the same as long-term care, and Heart to Heart treats it that way. Care plans are built around a finish line — getting back to the garden, the kitchen, the daily walk — with support that steps back as independence returns.

Steady support, with an end in sight.

In practice

Getting back to the daily walk, safely.

  • Medication oversight that keeps daily routines on track.

Common questions

Recovery questions families ask.

Can you help right after a hospital stay?

Yes. Our Recovery & Transitional Support is designed exactly for this — short-term, attentive support that helps clients heal safely at home after a hospital stay, illness, or diagnosis such as stroke or Parkinson's.

How quickly can care begin?

Timelines depend on your family's needs and the type of support required. In many cases, we can schedule a complimentary in-home assessment within days and begin building a care plan shortly after.

Do you work with hospitals or discharge planners?

Yes. We coordinate closely with families and discharge planners to build safe, well-supported transitions home after a hospital stay, illness, or procedure.

Need support soon? Let's talk this week.

Recovery care is time-sensitive. Message us and we'll move quickly to arrange a first in-home visit.
